# Code of Conduct

We want LoopKit to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience level,
background, identity, or tooling preferences.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or trolling
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban contributors for violations.
Report concerns to `<dhruvnarayan1023@gmail.com>`. Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
